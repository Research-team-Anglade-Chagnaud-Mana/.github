# Research-team-Anglade-Chagnaud-Mana

Three friends studying at École polytechnique (X): Max Anglade, Rami Chagnaud and Adel Mana. We do hackathons, plus more serious research projects in quantum physics and AI.

## Research

### [NeuralNetworkQuantumStates](https://github.com/Research-team-Anglade-Chagnaud-Mana/NeuralNetworkQuantumStates)
Code for our PSC (*projet scientifique collectif*) at l'X, supervised by Filippo Vicentini (CPHT). We use neural quantum states (NetKet / JAX) to study the disordered transverse-field Ising model in 1D and 2D: ground states, the quantum phase transition, and critical exponents from finite-size scaling.

### [FoundationNeuralQuantumStatesDisorderedIsing](https://github.com/Research-team-Anglade-Chagnaud-Mana/FoundationNeuralQuantumStatesDisorderedIsing)
Ground states of the 1D and 2D disordered transverse-field Ising model learned by a *foundation* neural quantum state: a single Vision Transformer trained over a whole family of Hamiltonians (field strengths and disorder realisations) instead of one network per system.

## Hackathons

### [ZulipHackJunction](https://github.com/Research-team-Anglade-Chagnaud-Mana/ZulipHackJunction) · *Needed*
Built at Junction 2026, for the challenge on home support after depression treatment by TMS. A community where patients in remission help others: an AI estimates the patient's state, picks requests that suit it, has Gemini write them, and the patient's reaction adjusts what comes next.

### [QuantumInsuranceResourceAllocations](https://github.com/Research-team-Anglade-Chagnaud-Mana/QuantumInsuranceResourceAllocations)
Allocating resources across correlated insurance claims, written as a constrained combinatorial optimisation problem and solved with QAOA / recursive QAOA, VQE (PennyLane) and neural quantum states, benchmarked on datasets from 12 to 100 claims.
